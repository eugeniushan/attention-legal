Delete your Attention account

Attention by eugeniushan. You can delete your account and its data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Attention and go to the Progress tab.
  2. Tap the gear at the top right to open Settings.
  3. Choose Account › Delete account, then confirm.

This deletes your account and cloud backup right away, and clears your progress on that phone.

By email

If you saved your progress with Google and can't open the app, email support.stillglow@gmail.com from that Google address, with the subject "Delete my Attention account". We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (the random id and, if linked, your Google name, email and photo link)
  • Your cloud backup: progress, sessions, coins, items and settings
  • Usage data linked to your account id, within 30 days of your request

What we keep

Purchase records needed for tax and accounting are kept by Google Play and RevenueCat for as long as the law requires, usually up to 7 years. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play first (Play Store › your profile › Payments & subscriptions).
