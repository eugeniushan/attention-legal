Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Stillglow is an app that helps you rebuild your attention span with short real-world activities and a companion called Tumble. It's made by Eugene Han Jr. ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, who helps us run it, and how to delete your data. If you have questions, email support.stillglow@gmail.com.

The short version

  • The app works without signing up. Your progress stays on your phone unless you choose "Save with Google" to back it up.
  • What you write in your journal and gratitude lists never leaves your phone.
  • If you turn on Tumble’s pause or screen time, the app reads which apps you open and for how long. That stays on your phone; we never see it.
  • We don't show ads, we don't sell your data, and we don't track you across other apps.
  • We collect anonymous usage data to improve the app. You can turn it off in Settings › Privacy.
  • If you saved with Google, you can delete your account and all its data in the app at any time.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your journal or gratitude entries, your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera, your advertising id, or which other apps you use. (Tumble’s pause and the screen-time card read your app use on your phone, with the Usage access permission you grant, and keep it there. Only counts, such as how many times you closed a paused app, go into anonymous usage data.) Reminders are scheduled on your phone itself, so no notification service receives your data.

To let you pick which apps to pause, the app reads the list of apps installed on your phone. That list stays on your phone too.

Like any internet service, our providers receive your phone's IP address when the app connects to them. We've set our usage-data provider to discard it and not to work out your location from it.

Who helps us run the app

These companies process data for us under their own privacy and security commitments. They may not use it for anything else.

  • Google Firebase (accounts and backups): firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play (downloads and payments): policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat (subscription status): revenuecat.com/privacy
  • PostHog, US hosting (usage data): posthog.com/privacy
  • Sentry, US hosting (crash reports): sentry.io/privacy

Some of these providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under standard data-protection safeguards.

How long we keep it

  • Progress and account data: until you delete your account.
  • Purchase records: as long as needed for billing, tax and legal reasons.
  • Usage data: no longer than 24 months.
  • Crash reports: no longer than 90 days.

Deleting your data

If you saved with Google, go to Settings › Account › Delete account in the app. This deletes your backup, your account and the progress on your phone. You can also ask us by email. Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your phone, including your journal.

If you never saved with Google, your progress only exists on your phone, and uninstalling deletes it. To delete the purchase and usage data linked to your random id, email us and include your Support ID (Settings › About, or use Contact support there, which adds it for you). It's the only way we can find your data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you can ask to access, correct, delete or move your data, and object to how we use it. Email support.stillglow@gmail.com and we'll reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Children

Stillglow is meant for adults. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has used it, email us and we'll delete their data.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and each account's backup can only be read by that account.

Not medical advice

Stillglow is a wellness app, not a medical device or treatment. The research notes in the app describe what studies found about each activity, not about this app.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app.
